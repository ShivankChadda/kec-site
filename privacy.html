Privacy policy: Purchase Desk

Effective 26 October 2026

This policy explains how Kailash Engineering Company ("KEC", "we") handles information sent to the KEC Purchase Desk, our internal WhatsApp number for construction purchasing.

Who uses the Purchase Desk

The Purchase Desk is used only by KEC staff: site engineers and supervisors, our logistics team and management. It is not offered to the public and does not send promotional messages.

What we collect

  • The WhatsApp number and profile name of staff who message the Purchase Desk.
  • The messages, photos, documents and voice notes they send to it.
  • Records created from those messages: material requirements, purchase slips and approvals, dispatches, deliveries and site stock.

Why we use it

To record what each site needs, what was approved and bought, and what arrived at site, and to keep accurate purchase and stock records for the company. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) carries the messages between staff and the Purchase Desk.
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads the text and photos of messages, only to turn them into purchase records.
  • Sarvam AI transcribes voice notes into text, only for the same purpose.
  • Google (Drive and Sheets) stores KEC's purchase register and purchase slips.

The records themselves are kept on KEC's own server and in KEC's Google Drive.

How long we keep it

Purchase and stock records are kept for as long as KEC needs them for its accounts and legal obligations.

Your choices

Staff can ask to see or correct the records about them, or ask questions about this policy, by writing to chadhakailash25@gmail.com.
